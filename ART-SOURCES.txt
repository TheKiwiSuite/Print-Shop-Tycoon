Approved equipment guide: the user's Level 2 Expanded Industrial Print Shop
illustration and close DTF-line references. The guide is retained in the Krita
project as a reference layer. The in-game scene is constructed independently.

DTF printer/shaker (v0.2.5): the five exact user-supplied PNG files from
October 12, 2026. Native assets dtf-line-approved/right/left/back/front.png
are byte-for-byte copies of the supplied images. No equipment was redrawn.
The connected main image replaces the rejected separate Blender renders.
Other supplied views are available in the station's Equipment Views panel.
The game mirrors the main image horizontally to align its film direction
with the existing isometric floor lane. Gallery images are shown unchanged.
Older editable Blender source remains development material and is unused.

Owner walking sheet: original generated chibi sheet created earlier in this
task before the user requested a halt to image generation. Four facing rows,
idle and walking frames. Used as animated owner/staff placeholder artwork.

Other machines, benches, props and toolbar character icon: illustration atlas
carried forward from the previous Print Shop Tycoon prototype. Its rejected
printer/shaker cells are not drawn by this native game. An unused equipment
generation that completed during cancellation was excluded from this project.
No further image-generation requests were made after the user's stop request.

Environment, product marks and interface: authored game drawing code.
Sound effects and ambient loop: original synthesized audio in game code.
Logo typeface: Barlow Condensed Black; bundled SIL Open Font License in
source/assets/FONT-LICENSE.txt. Body text: engine fallback font.

Art is not claimed to be a final exact reproduction of the reference style.
