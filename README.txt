PRINT SHOP TYCOON 1.2.0 — WINDOWS

PLAY
Extract the whole ZIP and open windows/Print Shop Tycoon.exe. No installer,
account or internet connection is needed. The version is shown in the
bottom-left footer. The build is unsigned: if Windows SmartScreen warns, choose
More info > Run anyway.

Start a New Shop and pick Relaxed, Standard, Expert or Custom rules. You begin
in your home garage with a gifted vinyl cutter. The left card walks you through
placing a heat press and the free artwork, inspection and packing benches, then
opening the shop. While that first job is in progress, other customers wait
(except on Expert). The Demo Shop is a separate save with equipment and staff
already supplied.

THE LOOP
Customers walk in with inquiries for products your machines can make. Open an
order to see the quote: estimated materials and expected contribution before
overhead and wages. Accept, approve the artwork, plan production, and work
each station. When Sam can do the next step by hand, a strip at the bottom of
the shop names it: SAM: WORK IT, or AUTO ASSIST to let Sam walk the prepared
steps for you (he still stops for decisions). Packed jobs are collected and
paid once. Every paid job earns XP and a star review.

Levels unlock machines: vinyl cutter, heat press, sticker print/cut, desktop
DTF, mug press, full DTF line, embroidery, banner printer, CNC and shipping
dock. Shop Growth names your next step, from garage to storefront to warehouse
(with its industrial fit-out) to the print factory. Your layout moves with you.

At the end of each day, PLAN TOMORROW shows bills, committed jobs, supplies,
service and the next investment, and lets you pick a daily focus (Balanced,
Volume, Premium quality, Customer growth or Recovery) with its trade-off shown.
Shop Growth > Review Specializations lets you become a Sticker studio, Apparel
house or Sign works shop. Named clients offer contracts with deposits and
bonuses in Customer Desk > Clients + Contracts; declining is always free.

Also in the shop: regulars, seasons, a rival across town, staff personalities,
your own merch line with an online store and weekend craft market, marketing,
equipment service and faults, optional timing events, daily decisions, Away
shifts with capped offline progress, decor, and local photo export.

CONTROLS
Move the pointer to the top edge or press F10 for the File, Edit, View, Window
and Help menus. Escape closes menus and panels; F11 toggles fullscreen. Close
saves before quitting and stays open if saving fails.
WASD or arrows: walk Sam. Click clear floor: walk there. E: open the nearest
station. Click a machine, customer or order card to inspect it.
Drag a worker onto a station to assign them; drag the yellow output token to
the next station to hand work on.
B: Build mode. Drag equipment to rearrange; R rotates eligible equipment.
Invalid spots are shown in red and nothing is charged until a placement works.
Wheel: zoom. Middle-drag: pan. Home: fit the whole shop.
Space: pause. 1 / 2 / 4: speed. F5: save. Q: capacity and cash forecast.
Tab / Shift+Tab and Enter work every on-screen button. Settings has movement
remapping, Reduce Motion, music and sound volume.

PHOTO MODE
File > Photo mode. Pick 1920x1080, 1080x1080 or 1080x1350; FIT SHOP TO FRAME
keeps the whole floor in the picture and HIDE CONTROLS shows the clean frame.
Exports are saved locally only; OPEN PHOTO FOLDER appears after an export.

SAVES AND UPDATES
The shop autosaves every 10 seconds, on exit and with F5. Saves live in
%APPDATA%/PrintShopTycoonNative with a .bak fallback; starting a new shop first
archives the previous one. Saves from every earlier version load.
Settings > Game updates + save backups checks the official release, downloads
it, verifies its SHA-256, backs up your saves and restarts on your command.
See UPDATING.md for manual updates and recovery.
